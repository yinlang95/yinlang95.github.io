About Publications

Lang Yin

langyin2@illinois.edu

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), advised by Prof. ChengXiang Zhai. My current research interests include reasoning for large language models, multimodal learning, and their interplay with theoretical computer science.

Previously I received my B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, an M.S. in Mathematics from ETH Zurich, and an M.S. in Industrial Engineering from UIUC.
